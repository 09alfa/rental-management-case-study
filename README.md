# Case study: 0→1 rental-management product (Lagos & Abuja)

*AI product management case study — in progress. Code and commercial detail live in a private repo.*

## The problem
Landlords in Nigeria, local and overseas alike, often can't see what is really happening in their rentals. When a manager or caretaker sits in the middle, rent, costs and repair history are reported second-hand, and the gap enables rent diversion. Most landlords run on WhatsApp, spreadsheets and paper, so disputes fail for lack of evidence.

## My role
Product owner and AI PM, working with Claude as an AI co-pilot for research, analysis and drafting. **All decisions are mine; AI supplied evidence, options and recommendations.**

## Progress

### Stage 0 — Intake (Oct 2026)
- **Idea selection:** scored six ideas, then four rental-specific AI concepts, on a weighted matrix (pain, AI-fit, whitespace, feasibility, willingness to pay, founder fit, risk).
- **Key call:** reversed an initial "AI-native" rule in favour of an **AI-ready core first**, with AI sequenced on the roadmap by data dependency. The first AI feature (WhatsApp intake) will still be specified, prototyped on a real model and evaluated during Design.
- **Separation by design:** core and AI requirements kept in separate artefacts so AI can be added, changed or removed without touching the core spec.
- **Segment:** defined by job-to-be-done (landlords wanting proof and visibility), not by location.
- **Constraints:** Lagos + Abuja launch, 8–10 hrs/week, £0 operating budget, core MVP targeted for end of March 2027.
- **Regulation surfaced early:** two tenancy regimes, a pending Lagos tenancy bill, Nigeria Tax Act 2025, NDPA 2023 data rules and WhatsApp's 2026 AI policy all shaped requirements before any build.

### Stage 0 gate (15 Oct 2026)
Passed with one recorded deviation: AI is sequenced after the core rather than shipped at launch, mitigated by specifying, prototyping and evaluating the first AI feature during Design. The gate included a stakeholder challenge (CEO on differentiation, engineering on scope, legal/DPO on compliance), each answered with a scoped plan rather than reassurance.

## What I'm learning (Senior AI PM lens)
- Platform and regulatory dependencies can shape an AI roadmap more than model choice.
- Pick the first product wedge that *generates* the data later AI features need.
- Autonomy is earned with evidence: start at "AI drafts, human approves".
